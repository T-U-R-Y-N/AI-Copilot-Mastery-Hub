    Confidence flags (T12)
    
            
    
              
    Green: supported by a source you can quote, or reproduced by a tool or second model. Use it.
    
              
    Yellow: plausible but unverified, or an error-prone fact like a number, date or name. Verify it first.
    
              
    Red: unsupported and consequential. Get a source or a person before it goes anywhere.
